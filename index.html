Sheet Music Training turns note and interval recognition into a practice habit you can do anywhere — even hands-free.

See a note (or two, for intervals) on the staff and answer out loud, or play it on a real instrument near the mic. Works fully offline, in English, Spanish, and Portuguese (Brazil).
