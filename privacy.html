Privacy Policy

Sheet Music Training · Last updated October 2026

This app does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer or any third party.

Microphone & speech recognition

The app uses your device's microphone to listen for spoken note names or played notes during practice. In most cases, speech recognition runs entirely on your device (offline) and nothing is sent anywhere. On some devices or languages where on-device recognition isn't available, Apple's built-in Speech Recognition service processes the audio on Apple's servers under Apple's own privacy policy — this app never receives, stores, or has access to that audio itself.

Data storage

Your settings, practice history, and personal bests are stored only on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

No analytics, no ads, no accounts

This app contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking SDKs, and does not require or support user accounts.
