Frequently asked questions

Why does the app need microphone access?
To let you answer by speaking the note or interval name, or by playing it on a real instrument near the mic. Audio is processed on-device and is never stored or sent anywhere — see the Privacy Policy.

Does it work offline?
Yes — question generation and on-device speech recognition both work fully offline.

Can I change the language or note naming system?
Settings lets you switch between Solfège (fixed or movable Do), letter names, or German note names, and between English, Spanish, and Portuguese (Brazil) for the interface.
